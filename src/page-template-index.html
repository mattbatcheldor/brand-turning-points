About this archive

Brand Turning Points started with a question about a Sony advert. I remembered thousands of coloured balls bouncing down a San Francisco street, and I wanted to understand why the idea had stayed with me for so long.

@@EDITION_WORD@@ editions later, it is an archive of @@TOTAL@@ cases from @@FIRST_YEAR@@ to @@LAST_YEAR@@. Every case has a source for its headline evidence and my own take, and most have a credited image and a film to watch. The series moves from how brands earn attention, in Fame first, to what gives people a reason to believe them, in Product first, and ends at the edges of the map in Edge first.

It is a selection, not a complete list. I chose each case to show something different from its angle, and there are many more turning points worth studying. It is also not a ranking. I rate each case on the strength of its public evidence, and that rating says more about what was measured and published than about what worked. Read the conclusions as hypotheses to take into a brief and test.
